The idea

Put your skills to use.

There are plenty of reasons you might be here. A layoff, a contract that ended, a break, the itch to build something of your own. They come down to one thing: you want to put your skills to use. Trouble is, so much of your best work stays invisible, locked under an NDA or lost when a project got cancelled.

So this is somewhere to use that talent, together, and walk away with something visible to point to. It fits around a job hunt or a day job. Dip in when you have the time.

Curious how it fits around a job search, or who owns what you make? It's all in the FAQ.

How it works

01

Pitch or join

Bring an idea you want to make, or jump onto one that's already underway.

02

Team up

Fill the roles you're missing. Sign up and join the Discord; build with the tools you already use and publish wherever you choose.

03

Ship the proof

Finish it, credit everyone, put it somewhere public. That's proof no NDA can take away.

Your profile

A profile that shows what you can actually do.

Everything lands on a profile that's yours to keep: a downloadable CV, the places your work already lives, and the projects you ship here.

It's your CV, plus all the proof a CV can't hold. One page to point a recruiter straight at.
