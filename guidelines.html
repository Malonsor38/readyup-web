Community Guidelines

Ready Up works because people trust it. The rules come down to three things: be decent, be legal, and only share what's yours.

One distinction up front: the work you build can explore violence, dark themes, and mature subject matter. That's creative work, not a rule break. These guidelines are about how you treat real people, and content that's genuinely harmful, illegal, or not yours. Mature art is welcome; being a jerk to real humans isn't.

Treat people right

  • No hate speech or discrimination: nothing that promotes hatred, violence, or discrimination against people based on ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any protected trait.
  • No harassment or abuse: no bullying, threats, intimidation, trolling, baiting, stalking, or coordinated pile-ons, and no abusive language aimed at real people.
  • No impersonation: don't pose as another person, studio, or as Ready Up itself, or set up a false identity to mislead people.
  • No defamation: don't post libelous or knowingly false statements that damage someone's reputation.
  • Respect privacy: no doxxing or sharing anyone's private information without their consent.
  • Keep it about the work: Ready Up is a place to create, not to debate politics or religion. Please keep those out of the community's shared spaces.

Protect people

Child safety — zero tolerance. Absolutely nothing that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers a minor, in any form. This is an instant, permanent ban and will be reported to the relevant authorities. No exceptions.

  • Nothing illegal: no content or activity that breaks the law where it's available, and nothing that helps others do so.
  • No real-world harm: no incitement or glorification of real-world violence, terrorism, or extremism.
  • No promotion of self-harm: nothing that encourages or glorifies suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders.
  • No shock content: no real or gratuitously disturbing depictions of violence or gore, and nothing posted purely to shock or disgust.
  • No sexual content involving real people: no nude or sexually explicit images of real people, and no non-consensual intimate imagery.

Respect ownership

  • Only share what's yours: only upload work you own or have the rights to use.
  • No IP infringement: don't use someone else's copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, or other proprietary work without permission.
  • No NDA or confidential material: don't bring in assets, code, or information you're bound to keep private from a current or past employer.
  • No plagiarism: don't pass off someone else's work as your own.
  • Disclose AI-generated content: if any part of your contribution is AI-generated, you must disclose it, as applicable regulations require, and the team you've joined has to approve its use before it goes in. If a team doesn't want AI in their work, that's their call to respect.

Keep the place safe and real

  • No malware: never upload or link to viruses, trojans, exploits, or anything built to harm people's devices or data.
  • No phishing or credential theft: no attempts to fraudulently gather people's logins, financial details, or other sensitive data.
  • No attacks: don't attack, disrupt, or try to break Ready Up's systems or another member's devices or accounts.
  • No account abuse: one real identity per person. No mass or automated account creation, and no accessing anyone else's account.
  • No gaming the system: don't fake endorsements, credits, reputation, or verification, or otherwise manipulate the community's trust features.

No spam or shady commerce

  • No spam: no junk messages, chain letters, MLM or pyramid schemes, or unsolicited mass advertising.
  • No harvesting: don't scrape or collect other members' data, emails, or contact info.
  • No gambling, trading, or soliciting: no gambling operations, buying or selling accounts, or begging for money.
  • Genuine recruiting, hiring conversations, and collaboration offers are welcome. That's the whole point of Ready Up. This is about spam and exploitation, not honest opportunities.

The work you make

Any kind of project is welcome: games, animations, software, tools, a wild experiment. If it's an idea worth building and you can gather the right people for it, it belongs here. Two things don't belong: crypto, NFT, or blockchain projects, and sexually explicit or NSFW content (off the table for now, in the work and anywhere else on Ready Up).

Mature and difficult themes are fine. Violence, horror, dark subject matter and hard stories are real creative work, and they're welcome. Two things keep that working for everyone:

  • Label it: flag mature or sensitive work clearly.
  • Keep it in the right place: mature content stays out of general, public-facing, and first-impression spaces.

The line is always the same: it's about conduct toward real people and genuinely harmful or illegal content, not censoring mature creative work.

If someone breaks the rules

We aim to be fair and to weigh context, but we'll act to protect the community. Depending on what happened, that can mean a warning, removal of the content, a temporary suspension, or permanent removal from Ready Up.

Some things skip straight to a permanent ban and, where the law requires, a report to the authorities: child endangerment, credible threats, malware, and other serious harm. Breaking a rule badly once can be enough.

Reporting something

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, tell us at [reporting contact / mod channel to add]. Reports are taken seriously and handled discreetly.

These guidelines will grow

Ready Up is early and built in the open. As the community grows and new situations come up, these guidelines will change with it. We'll keep them clear, and we'll keep them fair.
